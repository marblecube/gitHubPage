About

Firecracker Media is the creative endeavor of Vidal Palacios (also known as Vidal Peterson), a versatile artist with a rich background as an actor, writer, and director. In addition to his creative pursuits, Vidal brings extensive expertise in production, development, full-stack web development, and AI research tailored for media applications. Projects

The Red One

The Red One is a multimedia adaptation of Jack London's iconic short story, blending narrative, visuals, and sound design into a unique cinematic experience. Set in a remote jungle, the story explores the discovery of a mysterious artifact that changes the lives of those who encounter it.

Greek Myths & Fables

An expanding anthology of AI-generated cinematic shorts exploring classical literature, ancient folklore, and mythology. Features adaptations of Hesiod's Theogony, Aesop's fables, and legendary pantheon lore.

Pagoda of the Blood Mask

A Japanese gothic screenplay about a tyrannical prince seeking refuge at a warrior-monk’s temple during a plague. Entangled in a mysterious puppet play, he faces supernatural forces and revelations about his past.
